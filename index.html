ABOUT MARKET LAB

A market made by its traders.

Each agent combines a trading strategy with a decision-maker: a scripted rule, a local LLM-style behavioral proxy, or a human using the browser's single trading seat. LLM-style is not a live language model. Human orders enter at the next simulated minute.

One simulation step is one minute. Synthetic information has scheduled release times; automated agents process it after their individual reaction delay. Trades become observable signals after a delay, and agents can respond to those signals as well as prices and information.

The trace links observed information and other agents' orders to later decisions so you can inspect possible influence paths. This is an experimental record, not proof that one action caused another. Multi-user human sessions and live LLM connections are not included.
